UI/UX CASE STUDY · PYNEXIA TECH · WEEK 4

Booking a flight, without the stress.

Aerio is a mobile flight-booking app. You search, compare fares side by side and pick a seat on a real cabin map. You see every rupee before you pay, then fly with an offline boarding pass.

01 · THE PROBLEM

Flight apps make you work too hard to feel sure you got it right

A heuristic review of popular booking flows showed the same pain points again and again. The goal of Aerio was to remove each of them.

1

Hidden costs

Taxes, seat and bag fees appear only at the last step, so the "cheap" fare isn't cheap.

2

Fares are hard to compare

Fare names like "Lite" or "Value" don't explain what you actually get.

3

Confusing seat maps

Tiny seats, unclear prices and colour-only legends that some people can't read.

4

Scattered trip info

Gate, boarding time and status live in emails, SMS and the airport screens.

03 · USER FLOW

From "where to?" to the gate in 7 steps

The happy path keeps one main action per screen. The price stays visible in a sticky bar from fare choice to payment. Manage-booking actions loop back into the same screens (change seat → seat map, change date → calendar).

04 · WIREFRAMES

Low-fidelity first: structure before style

Grey-box wireframes fixed the information order on the four hardest screens before any colour was added. The tests: can you see the total price, the stops and the fare inclusions without scrolling?

07 · KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

Why it looks the way it does

💸 Total price is never hidden

The fare price includes the round trip. A sticky bar shows the running total from the fare step on, and the review screen itemises every charge before the Pay button, which repeats the exact total.

✅ Fares as a checklist

Each fare lists the same 5 rows (bag, checked bag, seat, changes, refund) with ✓ / ✕ in matching positions. You can compare by scanning down instead of reading.

💺 Seat map that works for everyone

Big 38 px seats, row numbers in the aisle and exit rows marked. Taken seats show "×", not just grey. Every seat has an accessible label ("Seat 14A extra legroom").

🚦 Status = colour + words

"On time", "Delayed 25m" and "Few seats left" always pair colour with text and a dot, so colour-blind users and glanceable reading both work.

📅 Prices on the calendar

Each date shows the lowest fare. Cheaper days are green, and a tip suggests a cheaper nearby day. This answers Nadeesha's question before she even searches.

🎫 One boarding pass, offline

Gate, seat, group, boarding time and live status sit on one card with a large QR code. A reminder shows when the gate closes and how long the walk takes.
